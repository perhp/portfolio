## Problems & Pain Points
**Core problem:** Companies need senior web and mobile development capacity they can trust for the long run, but agencies are expensive and slow, hiring is slow, and most freelancers deliver and disappear.
**Why alternatives fall short:**
- Agencies: overhead, juniors doing the work, account-manager layer between client and code.
- Offshore / low-cost freelancers: quality and communication risk, timezone gap, no ownership after launch.
- Full-time hire: months to recruit, high fixed cost, hard to scale down.
- Typical freelancer: short engagements, no continuity, knowledge walks out the door.
**What it costs them:** Missed launches, rewrites, code nobody understands, budget burnt on management layers, months of hiring delay.
**Emotional tension:** Fear of picking the wrong developer again; not knowing what is going on; being stuck with a codebase they can't change.

## Competitive Landscape
**Direct:** Other senior freelance web/mobile developers (Malt, Toptal, Worksome, LinkedIn), falls short because most optimise for short gigs and hand-over, and few combine web + Expo mobile + long tenure proof.
**Secondary:** Small dev agencies and nearshore studios, falls short because of overhead, junior staffing behind a senior pitch, and slower feedback loops.
**Indirect:** Hiring in-house or using no-code/low-code, falls short because hiring is slow and expensive, and no-code hits a wall for real products (payments, integrations, regulated data).
